## App Store long description — English

ZOTAIX is an AI concierge customization platform. Tell it what you feel, who
it is for, the scenario, and the budget — it answers with a structured
creative proposal: a liquid direction, a fragrance direction, bottle and
label design, naming, one line of copy that sounds like you, and a digital
identity mark.

The first principle: create and save a personalized object first. Then decide
whether it becomes physical. There is no cart at the door.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• AI Daily Concierge — a one-sentence emotional check-in returns keywords, a
light suggestion, and a shareable emotional card.
• AI Emotional Spirit & Fragrance — turn one emotion into a liquid direction
or scent direction with names and label copy.
• Bottle Studio — preview bottle, label, and packaging directions as design
versions you can iterate and compare.
• ZOTAIX Supply — the playful line: low-proof and zero-proof rations,
sticker-energy labels, badges, and card packs for everyday moments.
• Maison ZOTAIX — the premium line: enterprise gifting, client appreciation,
banquets, private celebrations, and brand collaborations, guided end-to-end
by a human concierge with quotation, samples, and delivery.
• Co-Creation Pool — publish a concept; 10 people open its public page, 100
bottles unlock flavor-direction review; founders earn engraved editions.
• Reserve — every kept object receives a ZOTAIX ID, a QR/NFC-bound
certificate page, aftercare status, and a replenishment entry. The archive
outlasts the bottle.
• Core Sequence membership — larger daily quotas, premium generations,
exports, and concierge access.

HOW DELIVERY WORKS

AI results are creative proposals. Real products require human confirmation,
supply-chain confirmation, age and region compliance checks, and a final
quotation. Small-batch customization means a standard, quality-controlled
base liquid carrying your personalized expression — we state this honestly
everywhere it matters.

RESPONSIBLE BY DESIGN

Alcohol-related areas sit behind an 18+ age gate. Public co-creation and
public archive pages pass human moderation. Profile tags like MBTI are style
signals — never diagnosis or gatekeeping — and are private by default.
Account deletion is available in the app under Profile.

Start with one sentence about today. Keep what it becomes.

## App Store long description — Chinese (中文)

ZOTAIX 卓序是一个 AI 礼宾式定制平台。告诉它你的情绪、对象、场景与预算，它会
回应一份结构化的创意提案：酒体方向、香氛方向、瓶身与标签设计、命名、一句像
你的文案，以及一枚数字印记。

平台的第一原则：先创造并保存一个属于你的对象，之后再决定它是否成为实物。
这里没有购物车开场。

你可以做什么

• AI 日常礼宾 —— 一句话的情绪签到，换来关键词、轻建议与可分享的情绪卡片。
• AI 情绪之酒 / 香氛画像 —— 把一种情绪变成酒体方向或香氛方向，附命名与瓶身文案。
• 瓶身工作室 —— 以设计版本迭代预览瓶身、标签与包装方向。
• 情绪补给线（Supply）—— 轻盈俏皮：低酒精与零酒精补给、贴纸风标签、数字徽章与卡片包。
• 高定礼赠线（Maison）—— 企业礼赠、客户答谢、宴席与私人庆典、品牌联名，
人工礼宾全程跟进报价、打样与交付。
• 共创铸造池 —— 发布一个概念：10 人开启公开页面，100 瓶解锁风味方向评审，
发起人获得镌刻版本。
• 档案馆（Reserve）—— 每个被保存的对象都拥有 ZOTAIX ID、二维码/NFC 绑定的
证书页、售后状态与补铸入口。档案比瓶身更长久。
• 核心序列会员 —— 更高的每日额度、高级生成、导出与礼宾权益。

交付如何运作

AI 生成结果是创意提案。真实产品需要人工确认、供应链确认、年龄与地区合规
校验以及最终报价。小批量定制意味着标准化、质量可控的基酒承载你的个性化
表达 —— 我们在每一个关键位置诚实说明这一点。

负责任的设计

涉酒区域设有 18 岁年龄门槛；公开共创与公开档案页均经人工审核；MBTI 等标签
仅作为表达风格信号，默认私密、可编辑、可删除。账号注销入口位于 App 内
「个人档案」页面。

从一句话开始，把它变成值得保存的东西。
